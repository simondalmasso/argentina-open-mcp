# Source policy profile: BCRA institutional website

**Review date:** 2026-11-03  
**Admission:** metadata-only; no bulk redistribution is enabled by this registry entry.

## Evidence

- Official Spanish legal notice: https://www.bcra.gob.ar/aviso-legal/
- BCRA statistical information entry point: https://www.bcra.gob.ar/estadisticas-indicadores/

The legal notice says its conditions apply exclusively to the BCRA institutional website and the content/services offered through it. Other BCRA channels and platforms may have specific conditions. Each API, dataset, linked external source and export must therefore be reviewed separately rather than inheriting this website policy automatically.

The notice allows reuse for informational, educational or academic purposes when the complete source is cited, the meaning/context is not altered and the integrity of the material is preserved. Commercial, advertising or campaign use requires prior express authorization. The notice was last updated in December 2025 when reviewed on 2026-11-03.

## Kernel treatment

The registry records this as `CUSTOM_TERMS`, not as a generic non-commercial license. The descriptor retains:

- evidence URL and review date;
- allowed purposes: informational, educational and academic;
- attribution requirement;
- integrity and non-misrepresentation requirement;
- commercial use disabled without prior express authorization;
- redistribution disabled by default pending a dataset-specific legal review.

The resulting status is `METADATA_ONLY`. The public catalog may expose the source description and official link; the source data is not eligible for bulk ingestion or redistribution based solely on this profile. This is an engineering safeguard, not a legal opinion.
